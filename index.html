I specialize in turning complex, jargon-heavy processes (like smart contract transactions, wallet states, and multi-layered data dashboards) into intuitive, high-converting visual flows. By focusing on progressive disclosure, clean structural hierarchy, and instant interactive feedback, I design interfaces that make advanced technology feel completely accessible.

Trust is not just a feeling; it is a designed interface. In platforms like Privacy VPN, I build credibility by establishing clear, transparent user states, visually prioritizing security status, and using clean, premium aesthetics (such as HSL-tailored dark modes and polished micro-interactions). Every element is positioned to reassure the user that their data is safe.

I design with a mobile-first, component-based mindset, adhering strictly to WCAG 2.1 accessibility guidelines for color contrast, typography scale, and keyboard navigation. I utilize clean grid structures and flexible auto-layout in Figma, ensuring the design translates seamlessly to CSS and maintains exceptional performance on any screen size.

Absolutely. I build robust, tokenized design systems in Figma using nested components, variables, auto-layout, and comprehensive property states. This serves as a unified "single source of truth," drastically reducing design debt, ensuring brand consistency across pages, and speeding up engineering cycles.

I believe great design is only as good as its implementation. I collaborate closely with engineering teams from day one. I deliver developer-ready Figma files complete with responsive spacing specifications, auto-layout configurations, defined style sheets, interactive states, and custom asset exports, and I perform visual QA checks throughout development.
