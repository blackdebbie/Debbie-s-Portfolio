Grooming Studio

A spa day, every day.

Our grooming process is calm, thorough, and personalised to your dog's breed and coat type. Walk in any day no appointment needed.

Walk-ins Welcome All Breeds & Sizes Mon-Sun, 8am-7pm Natural Products Only

Walk-in Grooming Journey

1
On Arrival, 5 min

Check-in & Assessment

Our front desk team welcomes your dog, checks vaccination records, and our groomer performs a quick health and coat assessment to personalise the session.

Vaccination Check Breed Assessment Coat Type Notice
2
Step 2 — 15 min

Pre-bath Brush & Detangle

We thoroughly brush your dog's coat to remove mats, tangles, and loose fur before bathing — this makes the bath more effective and comfortable.

Detangling Deshedding Ear Check
3
Step 3 — 20 min

Aromatherapy Bath

Using premium, breed-appropriate shampoos and conditioners. We offer soothing, hypoallergenic, or medicated options depending on your dog's skin and coat needs.

Hypoallergenic Deep Cleanse Conditioning
4
Step 4 — 15 min

Blow-dry & Finishing Brush

A professional blow-dry using a pet-safe velocity dryer, followed by a finishing brush to leave the coat fluffy, shiny, and perfectly smooth.

Fluff Dry Line Brush Shine Finish
5
Step 5 — 20 min

Trim, Style & Nail Care

Breed-specific haircut, scissor finishing, nail clipping and filing, ear cleaning, and optional bandana or bow your dog leaves looking immaculate.

Breed Cut Nail Clip Ear Clean Optional Bow
6
Final Step — 5 min

Checkout & Aftercare Advice

We review everything done during the session, give you a brief aftercare report, and recommend a home maintenance routine between visits.

Grooming Report Home Care Tips Next Appointment
