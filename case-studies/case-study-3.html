Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI learn my tone?
It analyzes a sample of your past sent emails to understand your vocabulary, sentence structure, and typical greetings/sign-offs.
Is my email data secure?
Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption to protect your data. Your emails are never used to train our public models, and we comply with all major data protection regulations like GDPR and CCPA.
Does it work with Gmail and Outlook?
Yes! Mail Nova integrates seamlessly with Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, and most other major email providers via our browser extension and native apps.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. You will retain access to your premium features until the end of your current billing cycle.
